If we're being honest,

I know where I want to grow

I want to work at a startup. Not because it's trendy, because I'm a builder, and even when nobody asks, my brain is already in "how do we do more." Being part of something from early on is what excites me.

Getting there meant thinking differently about job searching. The best roles don't wait on a job board.

Build the right network

Connect with founders and recruiters on LinkedIn, intentionally, to train the algorithm, stay present in their feed, and make sure my name shows up before I even reach out.

Get ahead of roles

Use the weekly funding list to find companies at the right stage, locate their career pages, and search for roles that fit or that could grow into the right fit.

Personal outreach

Reach founders and recruiters with messages that open a conversation, not close a deal. Get into their awareness, build a real connection, or land a phone call.
