via

a matchmaking platform built on trust and human connections

the why (?)

למה בכלל פלטפורמת שידוכים

I've always believed the people who show up in our lives are intentional.

The relationships we build through life, through human connections, through being our true selves - they mean something. Love works the same way. I trust human moments. And I trust that the right person is probably closer than we think.

That's why I built Via.

A place where your people come together. Where introductions happen across wider circles, between exactly the right people. A place that looks out for you — that gives you the space to show up open-hearted, with real intention.

the who (?)

מי רוצה בכלל שידוך

Via is built for two kinds of people:

Singles

Who are tired of swiping. Who believe in human connection over algorithms. Who want to feel that someone is actually looking out for them — and are willing to show up with an open heart when they do.

Matchmakers

Who naturally bring people together. Outgoing, caring, socially fluent — people who put themselves out there with sensitivity and genuinely believe they can help someone find love.

(sometimes they're the same person)

the how (?)

איך זה עובד

Via works the way real introductions always have - through someone who knows both sides.

A matchmaker brings their singles into a trusted circle. Singles see a curated feed. Not an endless scroll, just the people their matchmaker genuinely believes in. They express interest simply: a like for quiet curiosity, a super to tell the matchmaker "look closer at this one."

The matchmaker sees everything. They guide the process, suggest the right moment, and make the introduction when it's ready.

the work (?)

איך בניתי הכל

With no engineering background

I built the full stack using Claude, GitHub, Vercel, and Supabase.

Designed with intention

From naming and brand story to visual language and design system (UI/UX)

Believing in the community

I grew Via through personal connections, word of mouth, and social media.

the positioning

From the very first moment, I knew I wanted to build something different - a product that was exactly me. Someone who believes in human connection, in love, and in giving people a real chance.

Nothing out there was offering that.

So I built it myself.

The positioning built itself — people just like me had felt the exact same thing. I refused to call it a dating app. Because I knew what that implied: casualness, ghosting, disposability. Swipe culture has made it psychologically automatic - if it's not immediately perfect, next.

it's something else.
